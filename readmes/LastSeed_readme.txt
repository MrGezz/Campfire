===================================================

L A S T   S E E D

Primary Needs, Wellness, and Disease

For The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

Author: Chesko

E-mail - chesko.tesmod@gmail.com

===================================================

Version: 0.2 (Prologue)

Last Seed is the third of the Skyrim Survival mods, alongside Campfire and Frostfall.
This release is the Prologue: hunger, thirst, fatigue and vitality, food spoilage, and
the Provisioning skill, with the remaining systems still in development.

Requirements:

    Skyrim Legendary Edition or Skyrim Special Edition
    Hearthfires
    Campfire 1.13 or later
    SKSE (1.7.3+ on Legendary Edition, SKSE64 2.0.20+ on Special Edition)
    SkyUI (SkyUI 5.1+ on Legendary Edition, SkyUI SE on Special Edition)

Last Seed reads its settings profiles from Data\SKSE\Plugins\LastSeedData. Please do not
delete the files created there, or your settings will be reset.

Please see:

http://skyrimsurvival.com/

for the most current information about the Skyrim Survival mods.
