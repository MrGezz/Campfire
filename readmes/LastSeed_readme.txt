===================================================

L A S T   S E E D

Survival Needs and Diseases

For The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition

Original author: Chesko
Systems from: Aytrus (Last Seed 5.3, Nexus Mods 56393)

===================================================

Version: 0.3.0

Last Seed is the third of the Skyrim Survival mods, alongside Campfire and Frostfall: hunger,
thirst, fatigue and vitality, progressive diseases and alcohol, food spoilage and preservation,
water sources and waterskins, follower needs, and the Provisioning skill tree.

Last Seed 0.3.0 continues this repository's own port line (0.1 prologue, 0.2, 0.3). It brings in
the systems from Aytrus's Last Seed 5.3, which continued Chesko's original, and adapts them to
run with the Campfire 1.13 and Frostfall 3.4 ports on Skyrim Special Edition 1.7.104. See
LastSeed_changelog.txt for what this release changes.

Requirements:

    Skyrim Special Edition 1.7.104 with Dawnguard, Hearthfire and Dragonborn
    SKSE64 2.3.1
    SkyUI SE
    Campfire 1.13 or later (it installs PapyrusUtil, which Last Seed uses for its settings)
    powerofthree's Papyrus Extender (a build for your runtime; Last Seed checks for it and
    uses it for food keywords, food effects and reanimated-follower detection)

Recommended:

    Frostfall 3.4 or later (exposure lowers the Vitality you can recover to)

Last Seed reads its settings profiles from Data\SKSE\Plugins\LastSeedData. Please do not
delete the files created there, or your settings will be reset.
